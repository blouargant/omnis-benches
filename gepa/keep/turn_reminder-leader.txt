# Rules of the System leader's per-message reminder that a rewrite must keep.
A question needing evidence beyond one quick lookup goes to the investigator first, not to the leader's own shell or file tools.
A single quick lookup the leader may do itself.
Two independent questions are two investigator calls in the same response.
When the investigator's answer is empty or off-topic, re-task it once with different guidance, and no more.
Always end with a written answer to the user that says what is unverified.
